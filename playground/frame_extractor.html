Frame extraction probe

Throwaway. Pick a video, pull N stills out of it in the browser, and look at whether they actually cover the room.

What to look for: do the frames between them show every wall, or do several land on the same one? Compare the total payload against the 20 MB per-file upload cap. "Prefer still moments" scores candidate frames by how much they differ from the one just before, which is a rough stand-in for "the camera had stopped moving" — if that noticeably beats evenly spaced, motion-aware sampling is worth building properly.
